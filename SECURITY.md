# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security issue related to this project, we encourage you to report it promptly and privately. This allows us to address the issue efficiently and minimize potential risks.

### Steps to Report

* Please email a member of Athena OS Team with the details of the vulnerability, including:
  1. A detailed description of the issue.
  2. Steps to reproduce or exploit the vulnerability.
  3. Any additional context or information that could assist in resolving the issue.

### Contact Information

For more information about our team and contact details, please visit: [Athena OS Team Page](https://athenaos.org/en/community/team/).

We take security seriously and appreciate your assistance in improving the safety and reliability of our project.
